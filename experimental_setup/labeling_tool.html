Before you begin
Please read before starting. In each labeling task you will see an anchor signal and several candidates. Your job is to:
  • Rank candidates from most similar to least similar to the anchor by dragging them.
  • Mark any candidate that is not similar at all as rejected.

Sample 1 of ?

In this experiment we aim to capture human notion for similarity between numeric sequences.


To do so, we assign you an anchor sequence and 9 additional sequences. We as you to rank the sequences by their similarity to the anchor sequence. You can either drag each sequence to its corresponding rank or denote the sequence as "Not similar".


When you finish ranking, you can submit the ranking by clicking on "Submit & next" and move on to the nage page. It will download the ranking file as .json file. Please keep it. There are 11 pages of ranking overall, but you can decide to stop ranking at any page you reach. After you finish ranking, please send me all the .json files downloaded to your computer during the experiment, grouped into a zip file.

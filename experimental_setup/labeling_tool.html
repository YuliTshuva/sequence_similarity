In this experiment we aim to capture human notion for similarity between numeric sequences.


To do so, we assign you an anchor sequence and 9 additional sequences. We as you to rank the sequences by their similarity to the anchor sequence. You can either drag each sequence to its corresponding rank or denote the sequence as "Not similar".


When you finish ranking, you can submit the ranking by clicking on "Submit & next" and move on to the nage page. It will download the ranking file as .json file. Please keep it. There are 11 pages of ranking overall, but you can decide to stop ranking at any page you reach. After you finish ranking, please send me all the .json files downloaded to your computer during the experiment.
